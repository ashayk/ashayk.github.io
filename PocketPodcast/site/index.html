How it works

From one or more videos to a finished episode, with everything you need for publishing.

Each step is laid out. No timelines, no traditional editing, just speed.

Import

Multi-cam/separate speakers or one wide shot

Import videos from your Camera Roll or Files, two videos, three, a whole panel. With a single perspective video (or direct recording in the app @ 4k), Pocket Podcast finds the faces and splits the frame into one crop per person, so a single-camera setup still cuts like multi-cam.

  • All video content stays exclusively on your device. Always.
  • Export resolution automatically matches your best imported video settings.

Identify speakers

Analyse Preview

Default AI-based speaker identification and segmentation run directly on your device. Turn on optional ElevenLabs Scribe, plug in your key, and you also get a best-in-class transcription with every speaker turn timed. The app asks before it spends your credit. One hour of audio is processed by ElevenLabs in minutes and costs pennies. No video ever leaves your device.

  • Automatic detection of isolated or mixed audio when running on-device segmentation.
  • Optional ElevenLabs Scribe integration produces transcripts and supports downstream Clips and Metadata.

Assign

Select which speaker is assigned to which video

Automatic scrubbing to speech segments to help you decide in seconds. From here, the edit is automatic: the shot follows whoever is speaking, with reaction cutaways on long turns.

Preview and polish

Professional level post production

Preview the entire edited clip before you commit. Color grading with live histogram - Auto Levels - trim - and state of the art, AI-based noise reduction are all available. Levels, exposure, temperature, tint, shadows, highlights, saturation, denoise, sharpen. Loudness normalization during export to standard −16 LUFS (or a custom value) is a toggle in Settings.

Trim

Non-destructive trim during the preview

The filmstrip shows the cut, not the source, so you trim what the audience will see. Everything downstream like the transcript or the clips follows the trimmed timeline.

Export

Local rendering in h264 matching the best input resolution

Just give us a name for the project and the video, its transcript and thumbnail land there, as do the clips and metadata when you make them. One staging area for publishing.

  • Opens in a player when it's done; share from there.
  • Written to local documents and visible in the Files app under Pocket Podcast.
  • Share via the Files app or copy onto a PC using file transfer (Windows requires the free Apple Devices App).

Transcript

Every turn, named and timed

Just name the speakers and a turn-by-turn transcript is ready. Export to SRT, WebVTT or plain text.

Noisy rooms

Clean audio automatically.

Speaker identification is only as good as the audio underneath it. Before analysis, Pocket Podcast measures the signal to noise ratio (SNR) of your speech relative to the background, and if the recording isn't quite up to studio quality, we offer to fix it first.

Measure

A reading, not a guess

We sample audio across the whole recording and report the gap between the speakers and the surrounds. A studio or a close isolating mic measures about 35 dB and up; a camera or phone mic across a table sits in the low twenties, where it might affect speaker identification.

  • Takes a second or two, regardless of the episode's length.
  • Set the threshold in Settings (default = 25 dB). The higher the number, the higher the threshold to be considered 'clean'.

Clean

Clean audio throughout the workflow

If you clean the audio as part of speaker identification, the noise comes out before anything else happens, on the device at 48 kHz. The cleaned track then becomes the episode's sound: it's what the speaker analysis works from, what the preview plays and what the export writes. Nothing to switch on later, and no second pass.

  • Run As Is keeps the original, if you'd rather judge for yourself.

Single camera

One wide shot. A multi-camera edit.

No second camera? Point one at the scene. Pocket Podcast finds each face, cuts a crop around every person (with the correct aspect ratio for subscaling), and from there the edit runs exactly as it would with one video per guest.

Detect

One crop per person, placed by face detection

Import the single file and run Identify Speakers as usual. Once the voices are known, the app scans the frame for faces and stages one crop around each, a standard resolution one step below the source, so a 4K wide shot yields 1080p per person.

  • Each crop is adjustable: drag the handles if you want a different framing.
  • Speakers are assigned to crops the same way as to cameras.

Eyelines

Automatic eyeline alignment

Every crop is positioned so the eyes sit at the same height, a third of the way down the frame. Cutting between two people who were framed by hand is where a single-camera edit usually looks wrong; here the eyelines match by construction, so the cut reads like two matched cameras.

Cut

The shot follows the speaker

From here nothing is different: the edit switches to whoever is talking, with reaction cutaways on long turns, and the same colour grade, trim, noise reduction, transcript, clips and metadata apply. The wide source frame is shown below for comparison.

Clips and metadata

The episode is done. Now the posts.

Press Clips and the transcript is further analysed by AI (via OpenRouter with your key). Sentiment analysis produces intro teaser, clip, and long-form timepoints that are then edited out of the exported mix locally. One more step and metadata ready for publishing is produced.

Under the hood

One tool for everything you need to publish.

SPEECH

On-device speaker segmentation

Local AI models. No account, no upload, no waiting on a queue. Maximum privacy.

TRANSCRIPT

ElevenLabs Scribe, optional

Word-level timing and speaker labels for the full transcript, clips and chapters. One upload of the merged audio per episode.

SINGLE CAMERA

One wide shot, cropped per speaker

AI-based face detection with automatic eyeline alignment in the crop, so a single-camera setup cuts like a multi-camera one.

PICTURE

Levels with a histogram

Auto Levels reads the untouched frame, so pressing it twice gives the same answer. Grade, trim and noise reduction combine without surprises.

SOUND

Noise reduction and loudness

Optional, state-of-the-art AI-based noise reduction on the mix and normalization to a target LUFS on export.

FRAME

Thumbnail with every export

A composite of the speakers, cropped using facial recognition and written along with the export.

PodMerge for Mac

Same engine. PC workflow.

PodMerge is the desktop version. Drop the video files onto the window, identify speakers, preview, post-production and export using the same tools as the mobile version.

  • Drag-and-drop import; multiple videos.
  • Preview, colour grade and trim in a separate window.
  • Transcript window with SRT, WebVTT and plain-text export.
  • Clips and metadata with the same OpenRouter flow.

Your keys, your account

Cloud integration is optional and billed to you, not through us.

Speaker identification and the whole edit can run exclusivel on device. If you want a transcript, clips and metadata, paste your own ElevenLabs and OpenRouter keys in Settings. They're stored in the Keychain, hidden in the fields, and sent only to those two services.

Every request to OpenRouter is sent with data collection denied and processed with Claude Sonnet to maximize data privacy.
