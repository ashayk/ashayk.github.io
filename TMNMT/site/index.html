Tailor Made Notched Music Training

An evidence-based program for tonal tinnitus.

Tinnitus Aid Pro allows you to listen to your own music, filtered to your unique tinnitus frequency, to help you live with your symptoms.

How it works

An initial setup, then it's simply listening to your favorite music.

01 Find your tone

A guided test plays pairs of tones and asks which one is closer to the sound you hear. It runs over several sessions on different days, following the protocol developed and validated by researchers at the University of Münster.

02 Set the filter

The test produces a filter at your unique frequency. If a hearing specialist has already measured it for you, you can enter that figure manually instead.

03 Play your own music

Pick anything from your local library. Filtering is performed in real time as music plays, so nothing has to be prepared or downloaded first.

Your tone

Two ways to arrive at your tinnitus frequency.

One you can perform yourself, over several sittings. The other assumes an audiologist has already measured it.

The matching test

Compare two tones at a time: play Tone 1, play Tone 2, choose the one that's closer to your tinnitus.

  • Sessions are spaced at least 8 hours apart, and you can set a calendar reminder for the next one in the app.
  • After completing all the sessions, a frequency is calculated and applied to your music.
  • The two-tone forced choice technique has been validated and shown to be an accurate and convenient way to measure your tinnitus at home.2

Entering it yourself

If you already have a frequency from a hearing specialist, you can set it directly. The app asks for it twice and ensures the entries agree.

Mechanism

Effective Spectral Contrast.

The TMNMT technique relies on a precise augmentation of the audio to create the conditions necessary to evoke lateral inhibition. The exact mechanism is derived from a series of studies that explored the optimum notch width5 and edge dynamics6 required to maximize the effect.

Precisely matching the filter to the song

Every song is evaluated individually to create the best listening experience. Here, we see a tinnitus frequency falling within a lower frequency range that can use a less aggressive version of the filter to achieve the necessary notch depth.

Adaptive gain at higher frequencies

The original TMNMT was less effective above 8 kHz because songs simply ran out of energy to remove. Musical energy typically drops off rapidly above 10 kHz, leaving those with a tinnitus frequency in the higher ranges unable to use the technique effectively. Later research developed a way around this by simulating energy within these higher ranges. The technique has been further refined in Tinnitus Aid Pro to maximize audio quality while reducing noisy artifacts and is applied only as needed.

History

The app keeps a record of listening times, a daily and weekly average, and a simple history for you, family members, or clinicians to review.

  • History consists of actual music playback, not simply opening the app.
  • Everything is kept on the device with the option to share.

About

Dr Alex Shaykevich

Dr Shaykevich (MBA, PhD) is a co-author and the developer of the software used in several of the principal studies that confirmed the benefits of tailor made notched music including the double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial1 and the evaluation of the two-tone forced choice method for tinnitus frequency determination2. Tinnitus Aid Pro represents a consumer-facing realization of these tools and was developed in consultation with the inventor of the TMNMT technique, Professor Christo Pantev.
